    Questions

    Good to Know

    اکثر پوچھے گئے سوالات
    What pack sizes do you sell?

    Every product comes in 250g, 500g and 1kg NutriCrunch pouches. Gift boxes are filled with 250g and 500g packs.

    Can you pack a dry fruit that is not listed?

    Yes. Any dry fruit can be packed on order. Message us on WhatsApp at 0339 8402017 with what you need and we will confirm the price.

    How does ordering work?

    Add items to your tokri, enter your delivery details and tap Confirm. Your order opens as a WhatsApp message to us. Press send, and we reply to confirm payment and dispatch.

    How much is delivery?

    Delivery is free on orders over Rs. 5,000. Below that, a flat Rs. 200 applies. We deliver across Pakistan.

    Can I add a message to a gift box?

    Yes. In the Build Your Own Box section you can choose a ribbon colour and write a card message of up to 80 characters, in English or Urdu.
